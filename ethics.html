Ethics & privacy

How this study treats your data

TrustLab is designed so that participating leaves no trace off your device. This page describes exactly what happens to your responses.

1. What is collected

When you run the study, the following is written to your browser's local storage on this device only:

  • A per-session identifier (a random string, not linked to you).
  • Start and completion timestamps.
  • For each trial: which stimulus was shown, your decision, the answer you typed (if any), whether it was correct, and how long the decision took in milliseconds.

No name, email, IP address, device fingerprint, or account information is collected. No cookies are set. No third-party analytics or trackers are loaded.

2. Where the data lives

On this device, in your browser's local storage. It never touches a server, because there is no server. TrustLab is a static site (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) hosted on a free static host; no backend receives or processes your responses.

The aggregate dashboard you see under Dashboard is generated from simulated pilot data (a seeded pseudorandom process), not collected from other real participants. This is clearly labeled on that page.

3. Your controls

Immediately after finishing the study you can:

  • Export your data as JSON for your own records.
  • Delete every trace of your session from this device in one click.
  • Re-run the study as many times as you want (each session is stored separately).

Clearing your browser's site data (or using private / incognito mode) also removes everything, since local storage is scoped to the browser.

4. AI ethics

The "AI assistant" in this study is a scripted set of pre-written responses, not a live language model. This design decision is documented in the methodology and has an ethics implication worth naming directly:

  • Your prompts and responses are never sent to any AI provider. No text you type is transmitted anywhere.
  • The AI cannot make up a wrong answer that harms you because every response is deterministic and reviewed in the source code.
  • The study cannot become a data-farming pipeline because it has no way to send data off your device.

These properties matter more than they might seem. Many "chat with AI" web toys quietly forward everything the user types to a third-party API and log it. TrustLab was built explicitly to demonstrate that you can build meaningful AI-adjacent research tools without that trade-off.

5. Not medical or professional advice

This is a small cognitive research demo. It is not medical, psychological, or professional advice, and no result you see here should be treated as diagnostic or clinical.

6. Consent, plainly stated

By clicking through the intro on the experiment page, you are agreeing to contribute data that stays only on your device unless you personally export it. There is no need to withdraw consent through us because the data is already under your control — you can wipe it at any time from the results page.

7. Contact

TrustLab is a personal research project by Nhi. If you want to ask about the study design, request the source code (it is public), or report a bug, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
